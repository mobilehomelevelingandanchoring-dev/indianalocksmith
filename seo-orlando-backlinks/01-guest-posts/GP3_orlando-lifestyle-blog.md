It's 11:30 PM. You've just returned to your car in the parking garage near International Drive. Your keys are inside, the doors are locked, and your phone is at 4% battery.

In Orlando, this scenario plays out thousands of times a year. The combination of tourist density, theme park parking lots, high summer heat, and one of the most active nightlife corridors in the Southeast means lockouts happen here at a rate most cities don't see.

But the lockout itself isn't the real problem. The decisions made in the next 10 minutes usually are.

## What NOT to Do First

### Don't Call a National Locksmith Hotline

You've seen the generic "locksmith near me" ads that appear at the top of Google results. Many of these are national call centers that broker your call to the nearest available contractor — often unlicensed, usually running far behind on ETA, and notorious for "bait and switch" pricing: a $35 quote that becomes $150+ once they're on-site.

The Federal Trade Commission has issued multiple warnings about locksmith scams concentrated in high-tourism markets. Orlando consistently makes the list.

**How to spot a fake listing:**
- The business has no verifiable physical address (just a PO Box or "service area")
- The phone number isn't local (Florida numbers: 321, 407, 689, 786, 305, 561)
- No Florida locksmith license number listed
- Reviews are mostly 5-stars with no text, or all posted within the same 2-week window

### Don't Try to Break In Yourself

Coat hangers bend door frames. Slim jims trigger modern anti-tamper systems. And on post-2015 vehicles, an improper unlock attempt can lock down the ignition ECU — turning a $65 lockout into a $400 dealership reset.

For homes: breaking a window in Florida heat means immediate air conditioning loss, potential injury, security exposure for however long repairs take, and cost that dwarfs a locksmith call.

### Don't Wait Too Long in Summer Heat

Florida's summer temperatures create genuine medical risk during lockouts — especially if pets or children are involved. Heat exhaustion can onset in under 20 minutes in a vehicle. If a child or pet is locked inside a car, call 911 immediately, not a locksmith. Florida law (F.S. 768.139) protects bystanders who break a car window to rescue a child or animal.

For adults locked out of a home or vehicle without medical emergency: get into shade, stay hydrated, and call a licensed local locksmith immediately.

## Local Knowledge: Orlando's High-Risk Lockout Spots

**Theme Park Parking Areas:** Disney, Universal, SeaWorld, and LEGOLAND parking lots are massive, confusing, and increasingly patrolled. Most theme parks have on-site security that can assist — but they typically don't unlock vehicles and will refer you to a locksmith.

**International Drive Corridor:** High foot traffic, well-lit, and close to dozens of hotel lobbies where you can wait safely. The I-Drive area has strong cell coverage throughout.

**Airport (MCO):** Orlando International Airport has a locksmith referral service through ground transportation. The short-term garage is well-staffed and brightly lit.

**Downtown/Mills 50:** Late-night lockouts near bar districts carry higher risk. Call your locksmith from inside a nearby establishment rather than waiting outside alone.
