## 1. Lockouts Are Extremely Common in Orlando — And Extremely Easy to Handle Wrong

Rental car lockouts in theme park parking lots are so common that many Orlando-area locksmiths have specific pricing tiers for "tourist area" calls. The combination of unfamiliar rental vehicles, distracted families, summer heat, and long park days creates a perfect lockout environment.

**What most visitors do wrong:** They call the rental company. The rental company calls a national roadside dispatch service. The dispatch service finds the nearest contractor. Average wait time: 90 minutes to 3+ hours.

**What works:** Call a local locksmith directly. A licensed local locksmith in the Orlando area typically arrives in 20–30 minutes — often faster than the rental company's contractor even receives the dispatch call.

**Important:** Florida's summer heat makes vehicle lockouts a potential medical emergency if children or pets are inside. In that case, call 911 immediately. For adults: get to shade, stay hydrated, and call a local locksmith.

## 2. "Locksmith Near Me" Google Results in Tourist Areas Are Often Scams

The FTC has documented a concentrated pattern of locksmith fraud in high-tourism markets — and Orlando is consistently on the list. Here's how the scam works:

- A fake listing appears at the top of search results with a low quote ($35–$45)
- A contractor is dispatched who quotes a much higher price on arrival
- The traveler, in an unfamiliar area with limited options, pays

**How to protect yourself:**
- Look for a Florida state license number in the listing (search myfloridalicense.com to verify)
- Call a business with a verifiable physical address and local phone number
- Get a verbal quote on the phone and confirm it won't change before they start work

## 3. Your Hotel Concierge Is a Better Resource Than You Think

Most Orlando hotel concierges maintain a list of vetted, licensed local service providers — including locksmiths. If you're staying at a major hotel, your concierge desk can often connect you with a trusted provider faster than searching independently.

## 4. Know Your Rental Car Emergency Options Before You Need Them

Most major rental companies now offer mobile app access that can unlock your vehicle remotely:

- **Hertz:** Gold member app unlock feature
- **Enterprise:** "Let's Go" app with remote unlock capability
- **Avis:** Mobile app with unlock (requires prior activation)
- **National:** Mobile app for Emerald Club members

**The catch:** These features require prior setup in the app, active account authentication, and cellular connectivity. At 11 PM in a concrete parking structure with poor signal, "set it up before you need it" becomes very real advice.

## 5. Know Where to Wait Safely While You Get Help

Orlando is generally safe for tourists, but specific circumstances — isolated parking structures, unfamiliar neighborhoods, late-night situations — warrant extra awareness.

**Safe waiting spots while a locksmith is en route:**
- **Theme park areas:** Customer service buildings, hotel lobbies, security booths — all staffed and well-lit
- **I-Drive corridor:** Walk to the nearest hotel or restaurant lobby
- **Airport (MCO):** Ground transportation office on Level 2 — staffed 24/7
- **Downtown Orlando:** CityArts or Dr. Phillips Center lobbies during evening hours, well-lit public areas

Always tell someone your exact location — a family member, hotel, or the locksmith dispatcher — before settling in to wait.
