# Affordable Locksmith Orlando Expands Smart Lock and Access Control Services Across Greater Orlando Metro

**ORLANDO, FL — June 2026** — Affordable Locksmith Orlando, a Florida state-licensed locksmith with 5,000+ completed jobs in the Orlando area, announced today the expansion of its smart lock installation and commercial access control service line. The expansion adds certified smart home integration capability to the company's existing residential and commercial locksmith services.

The new offering addresses growing consumer demand for keyless entry systems, remote access management, and smart home security integration — while filling a gap in the market for professionally installed, properly secured smart locks.

**New Service Capabilities Include:**

*Smart Lock Installation and Integration:*
- Installation of major smart lock brands including Schlage Encode, Yale Assure, August Smart Lock Pro, Kwikset Halo, and Lockly Vision
- Wi-Fi and Bluetooth integration with Amazon Alexa, Google Home, and Apple HomeKit
- Door compatibility assessment and hardware reinforcement when needed
- Bore hole adjustment for retrofit installations
- Battery backup and emergency physical key setup

*Commercial Access Control:*
- Key card and fob-based access control for businesses up to 20 doors
- Keypad PIN systems with individual user code management
- Access log reporting for audit and HR purposes
- Integration with existing camera and alarm systems
- Florida Fire Code compliance review for commercial egress requirements

*Remote Key Programming:*
- On-site transponder key programming for 95%+ of vehicle makes and models
- Key fob cloning and OBD-II programming for push-button start vehicles
- Lost key replacement without towing for most domestic and foreign vehicles

"Smart locks are only as secure as their installation," said the company spokesperson. "We've seen too many cases where a premium smart lock was installed on an unreinforced door frame, defeating the entire purpose. Our assessments start with the door structure, not the device."

The expanded services are available across the full Orlando service area including Winter Garden, Apopka, Winter Park, Kissimmee, Sanford, Lake Mary, Altamonte Springs, and surrounding communities.

**Availability and Pricing:**
Smart lock installation starts at $85 (hardware not included). Commercial access control assessments start at $150. All pricing is confirmed before dispatch with no on-site surcharges. Service is available 24/7 for emergencies.
