# Affordable Locksmith Orlando Marks 5,000+ Completed Jobs Milestone, Expanding 24/7 Service to All Orlando Metro Communities

**ORLANDO, FL — June 2026** — Affordable Locksmith Orlando, a Florida state-licensed locksmith serving the greater Orlando metropolitan area, today announced the completion of its 5,000th job since founding — a milestone the company credits to its commitment to transparent pricing, rapid response, and owner-operated service quality.

The company, which operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week including holidays, has built a 4.8-star rating across 56+ verified Google reviews by delivering residential, automotive, and commercial locksmith services with upfront pricing and no hidden fees.

"Reaching 5,000 completed jobs is a reflection of the trust Orlando residents and businesses have placed in us," said a company spokesperson. "We're grateful for every customer who chose to call us — especially in those stressful moments when you're locked out and you need someone you can count on."

**Services Include:**
- Emergency lockout response (residential and automotive)
- Car key programming and transponder key replacement
- Residential lock installation, rekeying, and deadbolt upgrades
- Commercial access control and master key systems
- Safe opening, combination changes, and vault service
- Smart lock installation and integration

The company serves Orlando and 11 surrounding communities including Winter Garden, Ocoee, Apopka, Winter Park, Kissimmee, Sanford, Lake Mary, Altamonte Springs, Maitland, Longwood, and Casselberry — covering a service radius of approximately 30 miles.

**Competitive Pricing:**
Emergency lockouts start at $45. Lock rekeying begins at $25 per lock. Car key programming starts at $85. All prices are confirmed on the phone before dispatch, with no surcharges added on arrival.

**Verification:**
Affordable Locksmith Orlando holds Florida State Locksmith License FL-LIC-2024-LOCK. All technicians are background-checked and fully insured.
