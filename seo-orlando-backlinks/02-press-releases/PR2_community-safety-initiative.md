# Affordable Locksmith Orlando Launches Free Home Security Assessment Program for Senior Residents in Orange County

**ORLANDO, FL — June 2026** — Affordable Locksmith Orlando announced today the launch of a complimentary home security assessment program for senior residents aged 65 and older throughout Orange County, Florida. The initiative, titled "Safe at Home Orlando," provides qualifying seniors with a no-cost evaluation of their home's lock hardware, entry points, and security vulnerabilities by a Florida state-licensed locksmith.

The program launches in response to data showing that senior residents are disproportionately targeted in residential property crimes and are frequently unaware of lock hardware that has degraded below functional security thresholds.

**Program Details:**

Qualifying seniors can schedule a free in-home assessment that covers:
- Evaluation of all exterior door locks and deadbolts
- Strike plate and door frame integrity assessment
- Sliding door and window lock review
- Smart lock suitability evaluation
- Written report with prioritized recommendations

Assessments are available by appointment in the Orlando metro and surrounding communities. No purchase is required. Travel costs are waived for qualifying participants.

"We've seen too many situations where a senior resident's door was compromised because of a lock that hadn't been serviced in 20 years," said a company spokesperson. "A free assessment and honest recommendations — with no sales pressure — is something we're proud to offer to the community that has supported our business."

**How to Apply:**
Senior residents in Orange County may call (855) 633-0750 or visit https://affordablelocksmithorlando.com/ to schedule a complimentary assessment. Appointments are limited and offered on a first-come, first-served basis.

**Partners Sought:**
Affordable Locksmith Orlando is seeking partnerships with senior centers, community organizations, and faith communities to coordinate group assessment days. Organizations interested in partnering may contact the company directly.
