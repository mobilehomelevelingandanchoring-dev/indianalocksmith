## HOW SOCIAL BOOKMARKING INDEXES BACKLINKS

When you bookmark a backlink URL on social bookmarking sites:
1. Google crawls those bookmarking sites regularly (every 24–48 hrs)
2. Google follows the link from the bookmark → arrives at your backlink page
3. Google indexes the backlink page and discovers the link to your site
4. The backlink is now indexed and passing authority

**This is the fastest non-API method to force indexing of external pages you don't control.**
