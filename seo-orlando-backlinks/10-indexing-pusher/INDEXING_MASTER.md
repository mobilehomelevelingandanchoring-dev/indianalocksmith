## WHY LINKS DON'T GET INDEXED (AND HOW TO FIX IT)

A backlink has ZERO value until Google indexes the page it lives on. New pages can sit unindexed for weeks or months if you do nothing. These 7 strategies force fast discovery:

| Strategy | Speed | Links Indexed |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Google URL Inspection (GSC) | 24–72 hrs | Your own pages |
| 2. Bing Webmaster URL Submit | 24–48 hrs | Your own pages |
| 3. Ping Services (bulk) | 1–24 hrs | External backlink pages |
| 4. Social Bookmarking | 24–72 hrs | External backlink pages |
| 5. Social Signals (Twitter/X, etc.) | 1–24 hrs | External backlink pages |
| 6. Tier 2 Links (links to your links) | 3–7 days | Boosts authority of backlinks |
| 7. RSS Aggregator Submission | 24–48 hrs | Blog/Web 2.0 backlinks |

## STEP-BY-STEP DAILY INDEXING WORKFLOW

### Day 1 (As Soon As a Backlink Goes Live)
1. Copy the live URL of the backlink page
2. Submit to Google Search Console (URL Inspection → Request Indexing) — for YOUR pages
3. Paste URL into each ping service in `PING_SERVICES_BULK.md`
4. Post social signal on Twitter/X + LinkedIn using templates
5. Submit to 5 social bookmarking sites in `SOCIAL_BOOKMARKING.md`

### Day 3
1. Check GSC to confirm your pages are indexed
2. Submit remaining social bookmarks
3. Post Tier 2 article linking to the backlink URL

### Day 7
1. Check if external backlink pages are indexed (Google: site:URL)
2. If still unindexed: second round of pings + additional Tier 2 links
3. Submit to RSS aggregators if it's a blog/Web 2.0 backlink

## QUICK INDEX CHECK

To check if a URL is indexed, Google this:
```
site:https://[backlink-page-url]
```

If Google returns the page — indexed. If nothing shows — not indexed yet.

For bulk checking: use Ahrefs Site Explorer → Pages → check "Indexed" column
Or: use Screaming Frog with Google API to bulk check index status
