# TIER 2 LINK BUILDING — LINKS THAT POINT TO YOUR BACKLINKS
## Purpose: Tier 2 links juice up your Tier 1 backlinks, passing more authority to your site
## Logic: Guest Post (Tier 1) ← Web 2.0 Article (Tier 2) ← Social/Pings (Tier 3)
## Effect: Makes each backlink stronger AND helps unindexed backlink pages get crawled faster

---

## THE TIER 2 LINK PYRAMID

```
YOUR SITE (Target)
    ↑
[TIER 1 LINKS] — Guest Posts, Press Releases, Directories, Citations
    ↑
[TIER 2 LINKS] — Web 2.0 articles that link TO your Tier 1 pages
    ↑
[TIER 3 SIGNALS] — Pings, social bookmarks, social shares pointing to Tier 2
```

**Why this works:**
- A backlink from a page with 0 other links pointing to it = weak
- A backlink from a page that itself has 10+ links pointing to it = strong
- Tier 2 builds authority into your Tier 1 backlinks

---
