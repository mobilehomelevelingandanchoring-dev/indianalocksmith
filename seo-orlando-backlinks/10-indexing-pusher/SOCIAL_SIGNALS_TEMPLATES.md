## WHY SOCIAL SIGNALS MATTER FOR INDEXING

When a URL is shared on Twitter/X, LinkedIn, or Facebook:
1. Those platforms are crawled by Googlebot continuously (Twitter especially — every few hours)
2. Google follows the shared link → adds the target page to crawl queue
3. The page is indexed faster than any other method except direct GSC submission
4. Social share count is a secondary ranking signal (indirect, but real)

**Twitter/X is the #1 social signal for fast indexing.** A URL tweeted by an account with 100+ followers will typically be crawled within 2–6 hours.
