## Florida Burglary & Property Crime Statistics

- Florida recorded **approximately 120,000 burglaries** annually in recent years, among the highest rates in the United States (FBI UCR)
- **Orange County (Orlando)** consistently ranks in Florida's top 10 counties for property crime by total volume
- Residential burglaries account for **67% of all Florida burglaries** — with the majority occurring through ground-floor entry points
- The FBI reports that **30% of residential burglaries** involve no forced entry — meaning unlocked doors or windows
- Florida's property crime rate is approximately **28% higher** than the national average (FBI Crime Data Explorer)
- The average dollar loss per Florida burglary is **$2,800** (FBI UCR)
- Only **13% of burglaries** in the U.S. result in arrest (FBI)
- **57% of all burglaries** occur at residential properties during daylight hours (FBI)

## Automotive Lockout Statistics

- An estimated **4 million car lockouts** occur in the United States annually (AAA)
- Florida consistently ranks in the **top 5 states** for automotive lockout service calls
- **Summer months (June–August)** see a 35–40% spike in lockout calls nationally, with Florida's spike more pronounced due to year-round heat
- Modern push-button start vehicles (2012+) have made traditional slim-jim entry methods **non-functional** on approximately 70% of new vehicles
- Average wait time for roadside assistance lockout service: **60–90 minutes** (AAA internal data, various consumer surveys)
- A local automotive locksmith typically arrives in **20–45 minutes** in metro areas — roughly 50% faster than roadside programs

## Home Lock Security Statistics

- Approximately **34% of home burglars** enter through the front door (FBI)
- **22%** enter through first-floor windows; **22%** through back doors
- Grade 3 deadbolts — installed in the majority of new Florida construction — are rated for **150,000 cycles and minimal kick-force resistance**
- Grade 1 deadbolts withstand **250,000+ cycles** and significantly higher kick loads — the difference is $50–$80 in hardware cost
- **95% of kick-in forced entries** fail when a proper strike plate with 3-inch screws is installed (Door Security Council)
- Only **37% of Americans** have rekeyed or replaced their locks after moving into a new home (ALOA survey)
- **Smart lock adoption** has grown 43% year-over-year in the U.S. — but professional installation rates lag behind adoption, creating a significant security gap
